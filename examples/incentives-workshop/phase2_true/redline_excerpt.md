# True Act II redline — the three tracked passages (text excerpt)

The full tracked-changes redline was compiled against the authors' real LaTeX source
(`incentives.tex`, 45 pp) and kept in the authors' private session — the manuscript source is not
public, so only these three short passages (whose surrounding text is already public in the compiled
PDF) are reproduced here. Inserted text is shown in **bold**; nothing was deleted, and **no number was
changed**.

### Edit 1 — Abstract: design-stage-selection scope qualifier (answers F-057, F-066, F-044)
> …increasing financial rewards rarely produces large performance gains in the experimental settings
> most studied by economists. **Because this literature tends, by design, to avoid contexts where
> incentives are expected to have large effects, we read this as a statement about the contexts
> economists have chosen to study rather than about incentive effectiveness in general.**

### Edit 2 — Heterogeneity: foreground the dependence-aware averaging (answers F-086)
> …and orthogonalization of model space according to \citet{Amini2012}. **Because most studies
> contribute many correlated estimates, we treat the dependence-aware frequentist averaging as the
> primary basis for our heterogeneity conclusions and report the Bayesian inclusion probabilities
> alongside it for comparison.**

### Edit 3 — Publication bias: MAIVE exclusion restriction as an assumption (answers F-053, F-121)
> …can be expected to be less related to method choices. **We treat this independence from method
> choices as an identifying assumption rather than a tested property; absent a formal falsification of
> the exclusion restriction in our sample, we read the resulting correction as one member of our battery
> rather than a decisive arbiter.**

These three are the prose/scope must-fixes that the verification panel's `fix-safety` angle cleared as
pure-prose. The numeric findings were resolved by the actual re-run (see `REPRODUCTION.md`) rather than
by editing any number.
