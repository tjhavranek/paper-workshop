## The chair’s verdict

> BRUTAL READOUT. This is a beautifully engineered, unusually candid specification of a tool that, on its own admission, has never been shown to do the one thing it exists to do. The central claim — a 300-600-agent same-model fleet produces *trustworthy* review at scale and an Act-II rebuild that "hands back a corrected, reproducing manuscript" — is supported by zero measured evidence and, in four separate places, is retracted by the paper's own LIMITATIONS. The fleet is marketed as "independent" and "adversarial collaboration, not N independent reviews averaged"; LIMITATIONS concedes "every seat is the same model class... A fleet's agreement is not independent corroboration." The rails are sold as "checked by a deterministic script"; LIMITATIONS concedes "only the quote-gate is a real script today," and the one "genuinely enforced" tone-firewall is code-verified FALSE — {{REGISTER}} is injected straight into the severity-setting seats. Act II "re-runs your analysis" in the present tense; LIMITATIONS says "treat Act II as designed, demonstrated in part — and re-derive any number yourself." The honesty of that LIMITATIONS file is real and is the only thing standing between this manuscript and a pure overclaim — but disclosure of a missing keystone is not the keystone. Every corrective lives in a file the claim surfaces never cite (SKILL.md contains zero "LIMITATIONS" references), so on the surface a reader and the triggering agent actually consume, the strong claims travel without their qualifiers. The single load-bearing fact: a research-INTEGRITY instrument ships with no recall, no false-positive rate, no false-negative bound, and its only demonstration is the tool reviewing its own design — "a development pass, not independent validation." Forty High findings across eight genuinely independent fatal axes cannot all be true and the contribution still stand as written. The fix is not to delete claims; it is to right-size every claim to the evidence AT THE POINT each claim is made, demote the present-tense efficacy framing out of LIMITATIONS and into the abstract, and add the one cheap thing — even an n=1 seeded-flaw worked example — that converts "asserted" into "demonstrated in part." Until a measured recall/false-positive run exists, this is, in its own words, "one very thorough opinion," and the prose must live within that.

**Validity verdict.** NOT SUPPORTED as written. The central claim — that this apparatus delivers trustworthy review at scale plus a reproducing rebuild — fails on all four of its load-bearing pillars, each by the paper's own concession: (1) EFFECTIVENESS is unmeasured — no recall, no false-positive rate, and, more dangerously, no characterization or bound on the false-NEGATIVE (a confident green light over a fatal flaw), which is the exact failure the tool exists to prevent; the only demonstration is a circular self-audit conceded to be 'not independent validation.' (2) INDEPENDENCE is overclaimed — the binding constitution (rule 5) calls isolation 'the only thing that makes a same-model fleet more than N correlated draws,' but LIMITATIONS retracts it ('A fleet's agreement is not independent corroboration'); isolation removes induced herding (genuinely — the defender seat affirms this) but not shared-prior correlation, and the same-model verification panel cannot catch the shared-blind-spot error class that scaling 1->600 amplifies. (3) DETERMINISTIC ENFORCEMENT is overclaimed — exactly one control (quote-existence) is a script and it is the lowest-leverage one; the checks that decide validity, severity, and number-correctness are the same model class grading itself, and the 'genuinely enforced' tone-firewall is code-verified FALSE ({{REGISTER}} is injected into the severity-setting seats). (4) THE REBUILD is undemonstrated — Act II is 'designed, demonstrated in part,' has never gated a real revised paper, and lacks even a reproduction predicate, so the marquee 'reproducing manuscript' guarantee has no checkable meaning. Crucially, every corrective lives in LIMITATIONS.md, a file the claim surfaces never cite (SKILL.md, the text the agent harness consumes, has zero LIMITATIONS references), so the hedges do not in practice reach the reader who acts on the claims — converting honest disclosure into deniability. The genuine assets are real (a fail-closed quote-gate, herding removal, role-separated provenance, and the unusually candid LIMITATIONS itself), and the contribution CAN stand — but only if every importance claim is right-sized to the evidence at the point it is made, the present-tense efficacy framing moves into the abstract, and at minimum one seeded-flaw worked example is supplied. As the document stands, the truth of the central claim is not established, and validity — which dominates venue — therefore reads NOT SUPPORTED, independent of how the venue read lands.

**Venue read.** `desk-reject-risk` — ONE end-to-end worked example with SEEDED GROUND TRUTH on a third-party-style paper — reporting (a) whether the fleet surfaced a planted real flaw (recall signal) and (b) whether the panel rejected a planted confident-wrong non-flaw (false-positive signal), even n=1 labeled illustrative — combined with moving the present-tense efficacy framing out of LIMITATIONS and into the abstract/description. That single move converts the keystone claim from 'asserted' to 'demonstrated in part' and dissolves the genre disagreement: as an empirical 'this tool works' paper it stays desk-reject-risk until a measured recall/FPR run exists, but as a design/systems contribution that explicitly disclaims effectiveness up front, the same content survives — because a design paper is not required to carry an effectiveness measurement, only required NOT to imply one it lacks. The ship-vs-hold-submission decision is genuinely the author's; the abstract-level honest scoping is not optional either way.

**Kill shots (verbatim, desk-reject pre-mortem — routed around the chair).**

> This paper dies because it ships and markets a research-INTEGRITY instrument whose one load-bearing empirical claim — that it catches real flaws without manufacturing false ones — was never measured even once, and the paper says so in its own LIMITATIONS while shipping anyway; the instructive death is a CRUCIBLE-'workshopped' manuscript that gets a confident verdict, is published, and is retracted for a flaw that sat inside a range the tool certified as 'covered', at which point the indictment writes itself from the tool's own documentation.

> The paper specifies, in elaborate detail, the failure mode of a confident WRONG must-fix (the false positive), but never characterizes or bounds the inverse and more dangerous failure — a confident GREEN LIGHT on a paper that is actually fatally flawed (a false negative / miss) — even though that is the exact event that produces the 'published then retracted' embarrassment the tool is supposed to prevent; nowhere does the manuscript state what happens, or what the user is owed, when the fleet converges on 'competitive' and is wrong.

> The headline selling point — 'the largest possible Claude-only expert fleet' of up to 300-600 agents — is, by the paper's own admission, a population of correlated draws from one model that can share a blind spot, so the marquee scale is precisely the axis along which the tool fails silently; the paper dies the day a reviewer notes that 'the largest possible fleet' and 'a fleet's agreement is not independent corroboration' are the same sentence read two ways.

> Act II — the half of the contribution that the README claims sets CRUCIBLE apart ('an integrated rebuild that re-runs your analysis and hands back a corrected, reproducing manuscript') — is by the paper's own words only 'designed, demonstrated in part' and carries the instruction to 're-derive any number yourself before trusting it', which is a confession that the rebuild's core guarantee has never been shown to hold on a real paper; the paper dies when a referee asks for one end-to-end worked example and there is none.

> The 'completeness certificate' and the claim that the tool 'examined everything' rest on a coverage notion the paper itself defines as attention-not-correctness, where 'a flaw can hide in a covered range' — so the most authoritative-sounding deliverable certifies nothing about whether anything was caught; the paper dies the day a published, CRUCIBLE-certified manuscript is shown to contain a fatal flaw inside a 'covered' sentence and a critic quotes this exact admission back as 'they knew the certificate was hollow.'

## Central tensions the panel surfaced

1. Does same-model decorrelation actually produce independent corroboration, or does a 300-600-agent Claude-only fleet merely launder N correlated draws into a confident green light? The design both relies on the fleet as 'the product' (C-020) and concedes 'a fleet's agreement is not independent corroboration' (C-034) — the whole value proposition rides on which of these dominates in practice.
2. Do the named mechanisms actually deliver the safety properties claimed, or only their appearance? Specifically: does the deterministic quote-gate (C-008, C-043) plus quote-EXEMPT absence findings (C-041) close the fabrication hole, or relocate it to the unscripted absence/severity channels? And does the Runner/Scribe split (C-029, C-044) truly require 'two independent agents failing at once' (C-044) given both are the same model class?
3. Is the contribution honestly sized? The artifact is shipped and self-describes 'no measured effectiveness numbers yet' (C-031) and 'one very thorough opinion, not validated ground truth' (C-032), yet the marketing surface claims reviewing 'to the sentence' (C-001), genuine debate (C-014, C-020), and an integrated rebuild that returns 'a corrected, reproducing manuscript' (C-021). Does the honest-limits framing fully neutralize the headline overclaims, or merely footnote them?
4. Does the design break, mislead, or become theater at the scales it advertises (60-120+ seats, ~300-600 agents)? The batched-by-angle verification panel (C-011, C-046) is the load-bearing cost control AND the last line of defense against fleet-laundered errors — at quick tier it runs only three angles (C-046). Is the panel strong enough to audit a fleet far larger than itself, or does scaling the fleet faster than the panel invert the safety claim?

### F-009 · Medium · claim-support · S-METHODS

### F-010 · Medium · absence-silence · S-METHODS
- **Location:** SKILL.md 'What makes it trustworthy at scale' / shared_grounding_rules rule 8 (paper.txt)
- **Issue:** The manuscript states the panel is 'batched by angle' so cost does not explode with paper length, but is SILENT on the failure mode this introduces: a single blind agent must return one verdict per finding for a batch of up to BATCH findings (15 thorough / 20 exhaustive / 25 monumental; phase1_tribunal.js line ~173), so panel correctness depends on that one agent not dropping, mis-id'ing, or skimming any item in its batch. decide() fail-closes if NO verdict comes back for a finding (forces needs-author-confirmation), but nothing described guards against a batch agent returning a wrong/low-effort verdict on item 20 of 25, or returning fewer verdicts than targets with the remainder defaulting through. - **Why it matters:** Batching by angle is the load-bearing trick enabling 300-600-agent runs; if a 25-finding batch verifier degrades on the long tail of its batch, the multi-angle guarantee ('nothing reaches the user until checked from several angles') is weaker than advertised in exactly the heavy modes that batch most aggressively.
